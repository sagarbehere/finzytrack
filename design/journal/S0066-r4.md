# S0066: R4, the re-check after a save (BL-0152 part 1, with BL-0118)

## Brief
- Goal: after a save lands, its re-check (R4, `design/specs/engine-jobs.md` §6.7) runs, and the next save of that workspace is allowed only once R4 has let it go on; a mismatch pauses saving with the check-failed text, and only **Resume saving** lifts the pause. Today every closed record refuses the next save `WRITES_PAUSED {check_pending}` for ever (S0063).
- Base: 7b43ab10 (pushed, tree clean).
- **The split** (BL-0152 is too large for one slice, and half of it waits on work that does not exist yet). Recorded on BL-0152:
  - **Part 1, this slice (S0066-r4):** R4 itself. The digests; BL-0118's uncached loader; the closed record carrying the full post fingerprint and real digests; the engine ops; the R4 job queued after every landed save; R4 run first inside a save that finds an owed one; attempts and `could_not_check`; Resume saving; the build-change supersede (without its publish); `save_text`'s "could not check" variant.
  - **Part 2, later (BL-0152 stays open for it):** §7's reads and the open (the fingerprint re-check after the load, at most 3 tries, `LEDGER_CHANGING`, refusing on an `.open` journal), the focus check's rebuild, a save whose publish did not land queueing a rebuild, R4 at an open. None of these jobs exists yet (no `open`, `rebuild`, `preview` or `bql` work in the sidecar), and each needs the mirror publish (BL-0113, BL-0125: a formal model first, EP-19). R4's own publish of its `load_file` result (after a mismatch, a nondeterministic outcome and a build-change supersede; ADR-0010's amendment "adopts the `load_file` result") is the publish's too: `TODO(BL-0113)` in R4.
- **What part 1 builds:**
  - **Digests** (`engine/src/ledger_engine/ledger/digest.py`, pure): SHA-256 of a canonical encoding of a load result: entries in Beancount's order (`entry_sortkey`), ties broken by their canonical text; the errors (type, message, source, entry), sorted; the options map without `input_hash` (BL-0118: Beancount sets it from mtimes and sizes); file names in entry and error meta, `Document.filename`, and options `filename` and `include` as `path_key`s. Objects of any other kind encode by their fields; a `repr` fallback with memory addresses masked.
  - **BL-0118:** `beancount_port` gains a third class of wrapper, the reconciliation loads, over Beancount's own internal loader with no pickle cache: `reconcile_load(root)` = `loader._load([(root, True)], None, None, None)` and `reconcile_parse(root)` = `loader._parse_recursive([(root, True)], None)`; only `ledger/r4.py` may call them (`engine/tests/architecture/test_load_rule.py`); a test that no `.picklecache` appears.
  - **The overlay**: a parse digest of the parser's output (before booking, which builds new entries) when the caller gives a `path_key` function; a parse-only replica entry for the confirm, which also returns what it read.
  - **The save** writes into the closed record the full post fingerprint (its canonical encoding, so R4 can observe the same ledger set) and the real adopted and parse digests, computed after the commit's load and before the re-check (step 6 stays right before the commit point). The journal format goes to version 2 (`post_fingerprint` changes meaning).
  - **Engine ops** (`ledger/r4.py`, `protocol/r4.py`; each needs the engine lock; each acts on one named record in `<app_local_data>/local/<key>/journal/`): `r4_check` (steps 1-3, the real load: user code), `r4_confirm` (step 4: Beancount's parser and the replica's on the disk, the fingerprint before and after: parse-only), `r4_attempt` (one failure counted; the third renames `.mismatch`), `r4_resume` (Resume saving: `.mismatch` to `.done`). Every rename is followed by a sync of the journal folder before the reply.
  - **Sidecar** (`app/platform/engine_jobs/r4.py`): the R4 work shared by the background `r4` job and the save's step 1; the job queued after every landed save (`save_work`'s `after_saved`, wired by `submit_save`); the save's step 1: when the engine refuses `check_pending` naming a closed record, the work runs that record's R4 in the same job and then asks again in a fresh engine; the queue keeps an `r4` job whose engine died (as a save's), so its failure is counted in a fresh engine; Resume saving's work.
  - **Texts and codes:** `WritesPausedDetails` gains `record` (the record the pause comes from: Resume saving names it), `backup` (the save's backup folder: "Your backup is here") and `could_not_check`; `save_text`'s "could not check" text for the third failure.
- **The step rule** (written before coding). Every engine op may raise or its engine may die at any point; a power loss keeps or drops each unsynced rename. What each outcome rests on:
  - **`pass`** rests on, all in this order, in one engine: the record read from disk is `.closed` and decodes (format 2, digests computed); no `.open` journal of this workspace (matched by key or lineage, §6.5); the record's engine build equals the running engine's; the disk's fingerprint of the record's ledger set equals the record's post fingerprint **before** the real load; the real (uncached Beancount) load's adopted digest equals the record's; the fingerprint and the `.open` check again **after** the load, unchanged. Only then `.closed` → `.done`, synced, then "pass" is answered.
  - **`superseded`** rests on a moved fingerprint or an `.open` journal at step 1, 3 or after the confirm: the disk is no longer the saved state, so the prediction is not compared (spec: the next save re-plans on a fresh mirror). It never rests on digests. A build change or a record without digests (`not_computed`) is `superseded` after the real load and an unchanged fingerprint (spec §6.7 step 1; the publish is BL-0113's).
  - **`nondeterministic`** rests on: the load's digest differed with the fingerprint unchanged; then, in a fresh engine that runs no user code, the replica's parse of the disk read exactly the record's post bytes (its own record of the bytes it parsed), Beancount's parse digest of the disk equals the replica's, both equal the record's parse digest, and the fingerprint after the parses is unchanged.
  - **`mismatch`** rests on: the load's digest differed, the fingerprint never moved through the confirm, and the parse digests do not all agree. `.closed` → `.mismatch`, synced, then the pause is shown.
  - **`could not check`** rests on three failures to run the same record's R4 (the engine crashed, failed to start, broke the protocol or refused with an exception; the record unreadable), each counted on disk by an exclusive create of `<name>.r4-attempt-<n>` and a folder sync, in an engine that runs no user code. The third renames `.closed` → `.mismatch` and syncs. A Cancel counts nothing; no engine to count in counts nothing (the record stays `.closed`: the next save refuses `check_pending`).
  - **Saving resumes** only by: a record `.done` after `pass`, `superseded` or `nondeterministic` as above, or Resume saving's rename of the one `.mismatch` record the user was shown. Nothing renames a `.mismatch` record but `r4_resume`; `r4_check`, `r4_confirm` and `r4_attempt` act only on a record that is `.closed` when they read it, by an exclusive rename. **No path resumes saving after a real mismatch** (the confirm's `mismatch` is renamed before it is shown, and a power loss before the sync keeps the record `.closed`, so a later R4 runs again; it may then find `superseded` only if the disk moved, never `pass` on a different digest). **No path reports a mismatch as confirmed**: `pass` needs digest equality; a failed or unreadable step is a failure to run, never a pass.
  - **The save after R4** asks the engine again in a fresh engine; its own step 1 and step 4 re-read the records, so a record R4 could not finish still refuses (`check_pending`), a `.mismatch` refuses `check_failed`.
- **Actors in the test worlds** (S0065 lessons): an outside editor that edits, edits and reverts (A-B-A), and truncates then writes (`script > file`) during R4's load and during the confirm; an engine death at each op; a power loss after each rename before its sync (the fake file system); a forgotten and re-added workspace (records matched by lineage).

## Decisions
- **Split** BL-0152 into part 1 (R4, this slice) and part 2 (§7's reads and the open, the focus rebuild, the rebuild after a failed publish, R4 at an open): part 2's jobs do not exist and each needs the mirror publish (BL-0113, BL-0125, formal model first). Recorded on BL-0152 (agent; the orchestrator's brief allowed it).
- **R4 inside the save runs between two `save` requests**, not inside the engine's `save` op: the engine's step 1 (and step 4's re-read) refuses `check_pending` naming the closed record, writing nothing and running no plugin; the sidecar runs that record's R4 in the same job and asks again, in a fresh engine when R4 loaded (§6.1: "step 1 uses its own engines first"). The engine's own records check stays the guard: nothing about R4's outcome is taken on trust by the save. A record met twice in one save, or more than 16, refuses `check_pending` (no loop). (agent)
- **The background R4 targets one record** (the save's own, `<session>-<job>` in its key folder), not "the workspace's closed records": the sidecar must know the record before the load, so that a death can be counted against it. Other owed records (a forget and re-add) are found by the next save's step 1. No `r4_list` op was needed. (agent)
- **The closed record carries the full post fingerprint** (its canonical encoding) instead of its digest: R4 must observe the same ledger set, which a digest cannot give. Journal format bumped to 2; version 1 records were written only by tests (nothing in the product saved before), and read as `Unreadable` (an old-style record met by R4 is `unreadable`, a failure to run, so it ends "could not check" after three tries, never a pass). (agent; app-local data, not the ledger: rubric item 1 does not apply)
- **Attempts are files `<name>.r4-attempt-<n>`**, one per failure, created exclusively and synced, rather than a count file rewritten in place: the file-system port has no in-place replace for app files, and an exclusive create cannot tear. "Could not check" is a `.mismatch` record with the third count beside it. The spec's §6.5 row is renamed. (agent)
- **`.done {superseded_build}` carries no detail**: a `.done` record is history only, and the publish that would use it is BL-0113's. (agent)
- **The digest's errors keep their locations** (type, message, source, entry): ADR-0008's "error key" strips locations so a splice that shifts lines does not make old errors new; R4 compares two loads of the same bytes, where the stricter encoding can only call more things different, never hide a difference. (agent)
- **R4's load does not ask the `LoadPolicy`**, although ADR-0010's amendment says "the real `load_file` (through the `LoadPolicy`)": Beancount's own loader cannot be asked per file without patching it, and a parse-only pre-check would cost a parse (0.4 s at 54k) without closing the gap between the check and the load. Equivalence instead: the load runs only when the disk's fingerprint equals the record's, so it meets exactly what the save's policy-checked load met. With the desktop policy (allows everything) nothing changes; spec §15 names the gap for a refusing policy (hosted, not built, ADR-0041). The ADR's words differ from the code, so it went to the owner: `design/inbox/20261031-r4-load-policy.md` (recommendation: keep it; blocks nothing). (agent, pending owner)
- **A failure to run includes an engine refusal and an unreadable record**, not only a crash or a failed start: each is R4 not reaching an outcome, and each repeats; counting them ends in "could not check" (Resume saving) rather than a pause that never lifts. A Cancel and "no engine to count in" count nothing. (agent)
- **The queue keeps an `r4` job whose engine died** (`outcome_owed`, as a save's; queue table version 6), so its failure is counted in a fresh engine. The model counts it at the crash (`R4Failed`); the conformance replay maps an R4's crash to "finished" as it does a save's. (agent)
- **The nondeterministic notice says only its first sentence** until R4 publishes (BL-0113): "Finzytrack updated your views from a fresh load." would not be true yet. (agent)
- **Not built here:** the history trim of §6.5 (now every save leaves a `.done` record and a backups folder: BL-0169); the replay of R4 through the save conformance replay (BL-0168).
