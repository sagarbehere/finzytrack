## Decisions
- **ADR-0068 records D1-D8** (owner, 2026-11-01, in session: "I agree with 3,4,5,6, and 7." and, after the orchestrator clarified 1, 2 and the High, "Yes, go ahead with all of those"). Only D6a/D6b (process) and D8 (the queue) are built here; D1 is S0070, D2-D4 S0071, D5 S0072, D6c and D7 S0073 (BL-0178 to BL-0181).
- **D8 as built** (agent, within the owner's D8): the join is generic, at the scheduler. A second background save while one is queued joins it only when the queued work implements `scheduler.JoinableWork.joined` and answers a work; then both tickets are the one job's, and that job saves both requests' changes. Otherwise the second request is refused with its own `ENGINE_QUEUE_FULL` ("busy with other ledger work, try again in a moment": true, nothing of it was saved). Why not more: the price planner does not exist (BL-0177), and two fixed plans made on one fingerprint cannot both be written, so `save.submit_save`'s works never join. The rule applies to every `kind == "save"` submit that meets `AlreadyQueued`, whatever the entry point (`submit` or `submit_background_save`), so no path can hand a save another job's ticket; `replace_queued` now only ever replaces a queued R4 (it goes in S0070).
- **F9 as built** (agent): `_close` tells the worker of the job it leaves running past its commit point (`_Worker.close`); its `fresh_engine` then answers `EngineFailure("closed")` at once. Its `call`s still reach its own engine (it finishes, §4).
- **F3 wording** (agent): the registry's `WRITES_PAUSED` message is "Finzytrack could not finish saving a change, so saving is paused in this workspace." It is true for every cause: this save's unfinished change, and an earlier save's `.open` journal met at step 1 (where "your change" would name the wrong change). The next step (Reopen) is unchanged. §11's own texts (`save_text.paused_text`) were already right.
