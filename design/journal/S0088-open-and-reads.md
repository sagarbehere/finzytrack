# S0088: The open job and its publish; the publish's stamp fixes

## Brief
- Goal: BL-0152 part 2, with BL-0212 and BL-0213. Build the engine's `open` job of `design/specs/engine-jobs.md` §2 and §7 and the sidecar's submission of it: the mirror folder and its cleanup first (publish.md §4 step 1); this workspace's `.open` journal looked for before anything is returned (one, or a journal folder that cannot be listed: `WRITES_PAUSED {unfinished_save}`, ADR-0012 F-6, not tried again); a published generation that is already fresh (publish.md §8) ends the job (`fresh`: nothing loaded or built); otherwise the overlay load of the ledger's root (publish.md §4: "the open: its overlay load"; it records the fingerprint of exactly the bytes it parsed, globs included), then its publish through `ledger_engine.mirror.publish.publish` exactly as the rebuild after a save does since S0087, with the re-check just before the swap (the stamped set observed again, no `.open` journal). A moved fingerprint discards the result and the sidecar runs the job again in a fresh engine, at most 3 tries in a row, then `LEDGER_CHANGING` with Try again (§7, §11). An encrypted file in the ledger is refused, never decrypted (ADR-0059). This closes the loop of BL-0152 (S0087 red team O1): the open publishes, so a fresh install, a purged cache or an outside edit no longer leaves saves refused `mirror_stale` once the workspace is opened (the switch and boot that call the open are BL-0154, M2). The rebuild shares the code: its `fresh` answer now also waits for the `.open` check (§7: "before returning a result").
- Also: BL-0212 (V1: the rebuild's fresh shortcut also requires the hint's files, globs, documents roots and `document` files to lie within the generation's stamp; V2 and V3 tests; V4 `app.main.engine_command` resolves `--app-cache`); BL-0213 (the I4 integrity test moved to `engine/tests/` on the built API, its `fixme` dropped, I4 closed).
- Implements: ADR-0064 (decision 9: loads read without locks and re-check after the load), ADR-0068 D1 and D3, ADR-0015 with its amendments (the generation publish; the folder is ours), ADR-0012 F-6 as amended (a load refuses while a journal is open), ADR-0059 (encrypted files refused); `design/specs/engine-jobs.md` §2 (`open`), §7, §11 (the row "the ledger keeps changing"); `design/specs/mirror/publish.md` §4 (the open's publish), §8 (fresh), §11; EP-29, EP-30.
- Owner decision for this slice (2026-11-08, quoted in Decisions): the open writes and deletes only in this workspace's own mirror folder.
- Capabilities: CAP-DATA-MIRROR (stays partial: the screens' reads are BL-0126, the switch and boot that call the open BL-0154).
- Invariants touched: INV-05 (the mirror published atomically: the open is a second publisher), INV-30 (opening never writes the folder: the open writes only the app's cache), INV-31 (only the open workspace's mirror folder is touched), INV-32 (an `.open` journal refuses the open), INV-23 (log lines carry counts only), INV-10 (one load per try, the engine retired after it), INV-07 (the civil date comes from the sidecar).
- Risk class / lane: High / full lane (it publishes the mirror the save's gate reads, so it gates every save's availability). Verifier and a data-safety red team scoped to the new parts (the orchestrator's).
- The bytes it protects: no ledger bytes (the open writes only `<app cache>/workspaces/<key>/mirror/`). It keeps two outcomes truthful: what the screens and the save's gate read after an open is one whole generation built from one state the ledger really had (never over an unfinished save); and a generation is called fresh only when it describes the disk. And it never writes the ledger, the journals, `.finzytrack/` or another workspace's mirror folder.

## Decisions
- **Owner, 2026-11-08, in session, on the open's ownership**, choosing "Only its own mirror (Recommended)": "The open writes and deletes only in this workspace's own mirror folder, through the S0087 publish, exactly as decided. It never writes the ledger, the journals or .finzytrack/, and never touches another key's folder. Leftover folders of removed workspaces are left to the registry's 'forget workspace' (M2, BL-0102) and the OS's cache purge; a backlog row records it."
- **The open loads with the overlay load, not Beancount's `real_load`** (agent; publish.md §4 already said "the open: its overlay load"). The overlay replica equals Beancount's loader (pinned, `tests/ledger/test_overlay.py`), so the screens still show Beancount's own result (ADR-0068 D1), and it records the fingerprint of exactly the bytes it parsed with every `include` pattern, absent path and documents root. So the open needs no hint, its stamp has neither of the rebuild's stamp gaps (publish.md §11 "the stamp's globs"), and an edit during the load is caught by the re-check before the swap, not by a second observation. The rebuild keeps `real_load` (unchanged).
- **The `.open` journal is looked for right after the cleanup, before the fresh check and before the load** (agent; §7 says "before returning a result or publishing"). A `fresh` answer is a result, so it must not be returned over an unfinished save; and nothing is loaded when the answer would be `paused` anyway. Only a save of ours writes `.open` journals and none runs beside a job, so one look before the fresh answer suffices; the publish keeps its own look at step 7. The rebuild shares this (its `fresh` answer now also waits for the look; one new test).
- **Shared code** (agent): `ledger_engine.ledger.publishing` holds the target, the `.open` look (`unfinished`, fail closed on an unlisted folder, as before), the fresh check (`fresh_current`) and the re-check (`recheck`); `rebuild.Target` is an alias of `publishing.Target`. The protocol's target parsing (`protocol.rebuild.target_of`) and the publish's answer fields (`fields_of`) are shared by both ops.
- **BL-0212 V1's condition** (agent, wider than the row's wording): the hint must lie within the stamp on all four parts of a ledger set (files, `include` patterns, documents roots, `document` files; one under a stamped documents root counts as named), not only files and globs: a documents root the hint names and the stamp lacks has the same blind spot. Cost: a rebuild whose hint names something the generation never read loads instead of answering `fresh`; after a save the generation is rarely fresh anyway (the save changed the disk).
- **The open's tries are counted** (agent; §5.3's table makes only the rebuild after a save uncounted): a user waits for the open, and its engine's death ends it `ENGINE_STOPPED` with Try again from the scheduler.
- **The open is the switch's own job** (`owner="switch"`, waited-for): the queue starts it only inside the switch's or the boot's window (§3), which BL-0154 opens; the tests open the window through `EngineJobs.exclusive_began` (my first test version did not, and every open waited forever: the queue's rule held, the test was wrong).
- **`LEDGER_CHANGING` registered** (`Unavailable`, retryable, `data_safe` true, no details). **Pushback on §11's words:** "Another program keeps changing your ledger. Finzytrack will update when it stops." promises an update by itself, which is false for the open (it does not try again on its own) and needs the focus check for a waited-for rebuild (BL-0215). The registry's words are "Another program keeps changing your ledger, so Finzytrack could not read it." / "Wait until the other program has finished, then try again."; §11's row now cites `msg:code.LEDGER_CHANGING`. Agent-level user text (not rubric): flagged here for the owner's batch.
- **An open whose build was not published** (`inexact`, `incomplete`, `replaced`) is `Opened` with `mirror_ready` False (agent): the workspace can still be read, but saves stay refused `mirror_stale` until a generation is published; what the switch shows then is BL-0154's. An encrypted include is `EncryptedFile(path)`: ADR-0059's screen is BL-0154's; the path is never logged (INV-23).
- **Cut, each to a named row** (agent, as S0080 did): the focus check and its waited-for rebuild with `LEDGER_CHANGING` (BL-0215: no caller exists before BL-0126's container and routes; the engine half is built); the BQL job's §7 rules (BL-0216, M7 with CAP-QUERY-BQL); the preview's (a note on BL-0106). "The ledger facts the open needs" (spec §2) are not defined anywhere yet: the answer carries counts and the publish only; BL-0154 adds what the switch needs (BL-0166's write mode among them). No HTTP route exists for the open, so none was added.
- **The I4 test** (BL-0213): moved from `backend/tests/integrity/` to `engine/tests/integrity/` (the sidecar may never import the engine) and pointed at the open job; its arrange, act and assert kept, with two changes: the first publish is ledger B (so every later open meets another ledger and is never `fresh`), and the mid-build fault is injected into the build's postings rows (the build computes its rows before it creates the file, so a fault in the load result's entries would fail before any row is written).
- **Engineering principles:** no deviation.
- **Process slip, recorded:** while checking a test against the base I ran `git stash -- <rebuild.py>` in the working tree, which stashed the slice's own change to that file; `git stash pop` restored it at once (diff checked: 18 insertions, 41 deletions, as before). Nothing else was touched. Base checks are done with mutants or a scratch copy from now on.
- **Tests first** (run on the base 5893da6f before any code): `engine/tests/ledger/test_open.py`, `engine/tests/test_open_ops.py`, `engine/tests/integrity/test_mirror_publish.py` failed to collect (no `ledger_engine.ledger.opening`); `test_rebuild.py`'s V1 test and the `.open`-beside-a-fresh-generation test failed (`fresh` where `loaded` and `paused` were expected); the V2, V3 and "hint within the stamp" tests passed on the base (they test existing behaviour, BL-0212 asked for them) and are tagged `@regression-guard`; the later glob-half V1 test fails under the mutant that restores the base's fresh shortcut (`fresh-ignores-hint`). Backend: `test_open_job.py`, `test_engine_job_codes.py`, `test_engine_protocol.py` failed to collect (no `opening`, no `decode_open`); `test_main.py::test_a_relative_app_cache_reaches_the_engine_as_an_absolute_path` failed (a relative path).
