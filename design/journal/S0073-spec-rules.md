Base dfe3aee8. Two passes: **part A** (this implementer) the D7 code cuts; **part B** (to come: spec rules-only rewrite, fresh implementer) ADR-0068 D6c. Part A edits the spec only where its code change makes a sentence false.

## Brief
- Goal: BL-0181 (ADR-0068 D6c and D7, owner, 2026-11-01: "I agree with 3,4,5,6, and 7." and "Yes, go ahead with all of those"). Part A, the five D7 cuts:
  1. **Quit notes** (unbuilt): out of the spec's design and out of BL-0154's scope. Changes neither what a write does nor what it tells (nothing built).
  2. **Decision log:** only the content-free `sched.input` and `sched.decision` lines; the CI replay, the record-replay walk and the flight recorder deferred until a field bug needs them (the owner's requirement of 2026-10-26, narrowed by ADR-0068 D7). Neither line exists yet and they are not trivial (a redacting encoder for every queue event and effect, with a must-not-leak test under INV-23's privacy rule): **they stay in BL-0154**, whose row is reworded. Changes nothing a write does or tells.
  3. **Cancel ends only your own job:** the one rule goes (`app.platform.engine_jobs.queue.endable_by_another` and its use in `_cancel_queued`). A waiting user's Cancel removes only their own queued job and never ends a job running ahead; they read "Your request is cancelled. The earlier ledger work cannot be stopped from here; …" when a job of someone else runs ahead, as today. A read whose requester has gone still ends (`_client_gone`, unchanged). Changes what is **told** (the waiting user's answer) and what **runs** (a background save before its commit point, a background rebuild, or an open whose tab closed is no longer ended by another's Cancel), not what any write does: no write gains or loses a step, and a job that would have been ended before its commit point now finishes through the same durable order. The trade-off (a user waiting behind a long background rebuild cannot end it) is the owner's: "Keep B7 (Recommended)", 2026-11-01.
  4. **Process-group drain:** hold the exited leader (`waitid … WNOWAIT`), signal the group once, reap, never signal after the reap. The per-OS listing of members goes (macOS `libproc`, Linux `/proc`). **Gates a write** (it decides when the next engine may start), so it is the one cut in part A the data-safety red team looks at. The bytes it protects: none of its own after this cut; it was a second line in front of the engine lock. What keeps two engines from writing at once is the engine lock (§5.1): every engine takes it with a waiting `flock` after its handshake and before any ledger work, and the sidecar waits for it before every fresh engine (S0058). A plugin's member still exiting after the reap, or stuck in the kernel, holds the lock it inherited, so the next engine waits at the lock (with the advice on the timer, `engine_lock == "waiting"`) instead of at the drain. A member that closed its inherited lock descriptor holds no ledger lock and writes through no path of ours. What changes in what is **told**: a member stuck in the kernel with no job waiting no longer shows the "cannot stop" notice by itself (the leader is reaped, the slot is free); the next job that needs an engine shows it while it waits for the lock.
  5. **Records matched by `path_key` only, not by lineage:** **parked; not coded** (see "The lineage match: the bytes it protects" below). The scenario goes to the orchestrator, who takes it to the owner.

### The lineage match: the bytes it protects (PROCESS §3 step 2)
- **The bytes it protects:** a moved workspace folder's ledger files left half-applied by a cut multi-file save, which recovery cannot reach at their new paths; the lineage match keeps that folder from opening (`RECOVERY_BLOCKED`) and from being saved over (the save gate, `ledger.save.records`) until the user has seen the blocked screen and chosen Retry or "Keep the files as they are now".
- **Scenario where `path_key` alone keeps the wrong bytes without the user's word** (ADR-0063 decision 2 added the lineage match for exactly this: "Locate, 'Open folder…' on a moved or copied folder ... recognised by the workspace's registry key and the lineage id in its manifest, which the journal records, since a moved folder's files have new paths"):
  1. A save writes `main.beancount` and `2026.beancount` in `/A`. The power fails after the first swap and before the second: `main` holds the new bytes, `2026` the old (a torn set; the journal `.open` in `local/K1/journal/` names `/A/...`).
  2. Before starting Finzytrack again the user moves the folder to `/B` (or a sync client does).
  3. At start, recover-all tries the journal: `/A` is gone, so the path guard stops it (`unreachable`); the journal stays `.open`, blocking.
  4. The user opens `/B` by "Open folder…" (or forgets the workspace and adds `/B`): a new registry key K2, the same lineage id (the manifest travelled with the folder). A Locate that keeps K1 for the new path is still caught by the key match, which stays either way.
  - Today: the lineage match blocks the open; the blocked screen names the file and offers Retry and Keep.
  - With `path_key` only: the journal's path keys are `/A`'s, none is in `/B`'s ledger set, so `/B` opens silently on the torn set, and the save gate lets a new save be written onto it. Nothing is lost (the backups in `local/K1/backups/` hold the pre bytes), but the half-applied save is kept with no notice, which is what Keep does only after the user confirms it; INV-32's promise ("every unfinished save recovered before any workspace opens") is broken for that folder. A single-file save cannot tear (the swap is atomic), so the harm needs a multi-file save, a crash in its swap window and a move before the next start: rare.
- Not a matter of recovery's own tries: recover-all tries every journal whatever the match, so no try restores different bytes. The match decides only which opens and saves are refused.
- Hence parked per the orchestrator's rule ("If matching by `path_key` could make recovery restore or keep the wrong bytes, stop"). Options for the owner: (a) keep the lineage match (built; its cost is one field per record and two comparisons; recommended); (b) drop it by policy and say so in the move/Locate text ("a folder moved while a save was unfinished opens as it is"); (c) drop it and have Locate/"Open folder…" refuse any folder whose lineage has a blocking journal (that is the lineage match under another name). No journal format change is needed for (a); (b) would drop `lineage` from the record (format 5, older formats unreadable as S0070 did for format 4).

## Decisions
- **Cut 5 parked, not coded** (agent, by the orchestrator's stop rule): see the Brief's "bytes it protects". No journal format change, so no format bump; recovery's matching and the save gate are untouched.
- **Cut 2: the two log lines stay in BL-0154** (agent): they need a content-free encoder for each of the queue's events and effects (job ids, kinds, workspace ids, never a path or amount) and a must-not-leak test (INV-23); that is a small slice of its own, not a trivial line. BL-0154's row now names only those two lines and the deferral.
- **Background work is nobody's to cancel, so the waiting user is told it cannot be stopped** (agent, within D7): the base answer spared the "cannot be stopped from here" text when the job ahead was a read, an open or a recover-all with its requester present. A background rebuild has `requester_present` true (nobody ever leaves it) but no requester who can cancel it (`_cancel_running` ignores a job not waited for). Before S0073 the one rule ended it, so the case never arose; now it would have answered a plain "Cancelled." while the job ahead runs on and cannot be ended by anyone. `_cancel_queued` now requires the job ahead to be waited for as well. Same rule in the table's step property.
- **Queue table version 9** (agent): a decision changed (who a Cancel may end).
- **`cannot_stop_ahead` keeps its meaning** (agent): no API or contract change; the OpenAPI export is unchanged.
- **The drain's guarantee moves to the engine lock** (agent, within D7): after the reap a SIGKILLed member may still be exiting with the engine lock open (S0057 measured it on macOS: 36 of 40 runs). The next engine waits for it at the lock: the sidecar's wait before every fresh engine (S0058, `_engine_for`) and the engine's own waiting `flock` before any ledger work (`protocol.server`). Both are mutant-tested below. The client's end report no longer waits for the members (`test_a_plugins_child_holding_a_lock_is_ended_with_the_engine` now waits for the lock to be free after the report instead of asserting it at the report).
- **Told, after the drain cut** (agent): a group member stuck in the kernel with no job waiting no longer shows the "cannot stop" notice by itself; the leader is reaped and the slot is free. The next job that needs a fresh engine shows it while the sidecar waits for the lock (`engine_lock == "waiting"`). A leader stuck in the kernel still holds the slot and shows the notice as before. Spec §4, §5.2 say so.
- **Quit anyway with no notes** (owner, ADR-0068 D7): the spec's §9.3 step 3 now says the next start's recover-all recovers what Quit anyway cut, as after a crash; the "was saved / was undone / not undone yet" texts and their tests went from §14.
- **Spec edits limited to sentences the code made false** (orchestrator's rule): §4 (the one rule, its table row, the advice cases), §5.1 (spawn after the reap and the lock wait), §5.2 (the drain), §8.1 Afterwards (quit-note clause; lineage note says parked), §6.5 note (parked), §9.1 step 3 (one rule), §9.3 (quit notes), §11 row label, §12 note (the two lines are not built), §14 (Cancel, drain and quit-note tests), Orientation (Traps line on `killpg`, the D7 summary). §12's replay and flight-recorder bullets stay for part B under their existing "not the design" note.
- **Retired terms** (agent): activated "the one rule", `endable_by_another`, "no live member", `live_member`, `live_member_in_group_listing`, `live_member_in_proc`, the `libproc` listing names and "quit notes". Not activated: the flight recorder and the decision log's replay (deferred, not dropped: BL-0154 still names them as deferred) and the lineage match (parked). Two spec lines were reworded rather than allowlisted ("Quit anyway writes nothing for the next start to answer").
- **BL-0185 grep** (agent), over `engine/src`, `backend/src`, `scripts`, `formal`, for `one rule|endable|live member|live_member|libproc|proc_listpids|quit[ _-]notes?|next-start note|flight recorder|replay test|/proc\b|until it has no` (case-insensitive; the retired-terms check and its test excluded): 6 hits; fixed 3 (`ledger_engine.ledger.recover_all`, `app.platform.engine_jobs.recovery`, `scheduler` docstrings); left 3 in `formal/tla/engine-jobs/MAPPING.md` §5 (history, exempt). Earlier, the stale "no live member" docstrings in `app.platform.engine`, `engine_jobs.queue` (`EngineEnded`, `EndEngine`) and `adapters.engine_client` were fixed with the code.
- **Model** (agent): nothing changed is in `formal/tla/engine-jobs/MAPPING.md` (the queue, Cancel and the engine's processes are outside the durability model, its §3 row); the model and its small tier were not run.
