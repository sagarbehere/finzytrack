## Decisions
- **The section numbers stay** (agent): about 300 lines outside the spec cite "§10", "§11" or "§15" of it or of another document (inventory: `git grep -nP "§\s?1[015](?!\d)"` outside journals, minus PROCESS.md's and ARCHITECTURE.md's own sections), among them code docstrings, ADRs, failure-modes, the backlog, MAPPING.md and `gen_cfgs.py`. Every citation of §10, §11 or §15 therefore still leads to the content, through at most one pointer; none was edited. `test_engine_jobs_spec.py::Sections` pins the 34 headings as they stood at 3ce7b5bf.
- **How the texts are generated** (agent): `backend/scripts/gen_engine_job_texts.py`, after `gen_statecharts.py`, renders `design/generated/engine-job-texts.md` by calling the code's public text functions (and two private helpers of `recovery_text`, `_missing_now` and `_missing_kept`, so the "missing file" sentences show on their own rather than repeated inside whole screens) with placeholders (`<file>`, `<path>/<file>` where the code names a full path, `<workspace>`, `<folder>`, `<kept copy>`, `<entry>`). The system's answer (`strerror`) differs by OS, so `recovery_text.system_answer` is patched to `<system's answer>` during rendering. The registry's message and next step for the 11 engine-job and workspace codes are rendered too: `save_text.text` and the API show them when no row has its own words. Each text has a stable key `msg:<area>.<case>`; the spec cites the key, never the words. Registered in `scripts/check_generated.py` with the whole `engine_jobs/` and `errors/` folders, `engine.py` and the generator as sources.
- **A text not built yet stays quoted once** (agent): in §11, or in its rule (§8.2's create and copy texts, §9.3's shell texts), with the backlog item that builds it. Moved so each has one place: §9.1's switching texts and §9.3's quit confirmations now live only in §11's rows; §7's `LEDGER_CHANGING` words and §8.2 step 1's "not empty" words only in §11.
