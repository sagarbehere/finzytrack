# S0059: Model-driven conformance: TLC traces replayed through the queue table and the scheduler shell

## Brief
- Goal: the last of BL-0150's three slices. TLC-simulated traces of small worlds of `formal/tla/engine-jobs/EngineJobs.tla` are projected onto the queue's state (`QueueState`) and replayed through the queue table (`app.platform.engine_jobs.queue.transition`) and through the scheduler shell (`app.platform.engine_jobs.scheduler.EngineJobs`) over the fake engine port (`backend/tests/platform/fake_job_engine.py`). At each settled step the table and the shell must reach the model's projected state, or one the projection names as equivalent. Scope: the queue- and engine-level actions BL-0150 built (queue, dispatch, Cancel, a closed tab, engine start and death, the drain, the engine lock, the stop bound and its window, Try again, the timer's advice). Actions of later pieces are projected away, played through the shell's seam, or left out of the traces, each with its backlog ID (table below).
  - **Generator** `formal/tla/engine-jobs/gen_traces.py` (stdlib only; needs Java 21 and the pinned `tla2tools.jar` 1.7.4 of `formal/tla/tools.lock`): writes a trace module `EngineJobsTrace` beside a copy of the model in a temporary folder and runs `tlc2.TLC -simulate file=…,num=… -depth … -seed 20261029 -workers 1`. The trace module adds one variable, `act` (each step's action and its argument), takes the app's own dispatch (`Dispatch`, `DispatchUnavail`, `SpawnStep`) first whenever it is enabled, and leaves out the actions and branches of later pieces. Every step it takes is a step of the model's `Next`, so every trace is a behaviour of `Spec`. A small reader of TLC's values (records, sequences, sets, strings, integers, booleans; anything else refused) keeps per state only what the replay reads.
  - **Corpus** `formal/tla/engine-jobs/traces/corpus.json` (one JSON header line, then one trace per line): 7 worlds, 62 traces, 1,477 steps, 476 KB. The header records the SHA-256 of the model, of each base world's `.cfg`, of the generator and of the pinned jar (each under a `sha256` key, which the privacy scan reads as a hash pin), plus the seed and the world table. The command: `python3 formal/tla/engine-jobs/gen_traces.py` (about 15 s); `--check` compares the header without Java. Generated twice: byte-identical.
  - **Replay** `backend/tests/platform/test_engine_jobs_conformance.py` (no Java; the whole file runs in about 0.6 s). Each model step becomes ops (a request, an engine report, a time event, the lock coming free, or the running job's own part: its end, its commit point, its ask for a fresh engine, its engine's death). Two backends apply them: the table, fed the events the shell would feed it, and the shell, with a clock that moves only when the replay moves it, a lock wait the replay opens, a scripted work per job and the fake engine holding every end report until the model's `Drain`. Compared at each settled step: the projected view (queued jobs in order, the running job with its commit point and its ask for an engine, the slot, unavailable, the timer's advice, the open workspace), how every ended job was answered (finished, `JOB_CANCELLED` with or without "cannot be stopped from here", `ENGINE_STOPPED`/`CHANGE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, `ENGINE_UNAVAILABLE`), and the number of fresh engines started (§5.1 reuse).
  - **Currency check**: `test_the_corpus_is_current_with_the_model` fails when the model, a world's `.cfg`, the generator or the pinned jar differs from the header, with the regeneration command in its message; `scripts/tests/test_tla_traces.py` checks the same through the generator's `--check` path, plus the trace module and the reader.
- Implements: spec engine-jobs.md §13 ("the model-driven conformance tests", via BL-0150) and §14's queue, Cancel and engine rows as replayed behaviour; ARCHITECTURE.md §8.3 "Code mapping" (the limit `test_tla_mapping.py` names: a marker on the wrong function passes; the replay catches a wrong function by behaviour); ADR-0064; EP-19 and IMP-0045 (the model's findings become tests).
- Capabilities: `CAP-DATA-ENGINE-JOBS` (stays planned; the new test is listed on it).
- Invariants touched: none changed. The replay checks behaviour behind INV-10 (bounded queue), INV-01/INV-22 (the commit point: `Proceed` only from the table's step; no Cancel ends a job past it), INV-28 (only the open workspace's jobs start); INV-31's window is not compared (below).
- Risk class / lane: **Low / light** (test-only and model tooling: no product code changes). Its checks guard the queue's decisions on writes (Cancel's one rule, the commit point), so the loosening mutants were run anyway. The verifier may raise the class.
- Acceptance tests (each new, so each fails on base 967c06aa: the files, the corpus and `FakeEngine.hold_ends` do not exist there):
  - `backend/tests/platform/test_engine_jobs_conformance.py`: `test_the_corpus_is_current_with_the_model`, `test_every_action_of_the_model_is_classified`, `test_the_corpus_covers_every_mapped_action`, `test_the_table_follows_the_model`, `test_the_shell_follows_the_model[<world>]` (7 worlds), `test_a_divergence_is_caught`, `test_the_ops_reader_refuses_an_unknown_action`;
  - `scripts/tests/test_tla_traces.py` (11): the reader's shapes and refusals, a trace read keeping only the replay's variables, the trace module (every action but the left-out ones labelled once, the dispatch first, the guards applied, unknown names refused), and staleness.

Branches of a mapped or seam action that belong to a later piece stop their trace (`STOPS` in the test); the trace module leaves each out (`gen_traces.GUARDS`), so none occurs, and the coverage test asserts that: an engine death or a Cancel after the listing while recover-all runs (it goes on with the next journal, BL-0151), a `SpawnStep` from `rc_spawn` (BL-0151), an engine death of the open job and an `Assess` that queues the open job (BL-0152), and an outside edit during the boot (it makes the boot's open a job, BL-0152).

## Decisions
- **The app's dispatch first** (agent): the trace module takes `Dispatch`, `DispatchUnavail` or `SpawnStep` whenever one is enabled. The table dispatches inside the step of the event that allows it (§3: "after every event"), so a model interleaving in which the user cancels a job that could already have started has no counterpart in the code. The traces stay behaviours of `Spec` (each step is a `Next` step); the replay compares only at states not followed by a dispatch step, where the model has caught up with the table. Rejected: comparing every state (the table is one step ahead after every enabling event), and matching a run of model dispatch steps to the table's one step by search (needless: the run is consecutive under this rule).
- **Left out rather than stopped** (agent): the brief allowed either. Actions wholly owned by BL-0153/BL-0154 and the later pieces' branches of mapped actions are left out of the trace module's relation, so TLC takes another enabled step instead of ending the trace there; the test's `Stop` rules remain and the coverage test fails if a trace ever reaches one.
- **Worlds** (agent): seven, all small-tier: `S_queue` (the boot's own Cancel left out, so traces reach the open workspace's jobs), `S_queue_done` (`S_queue` without crashes, every Cancel left out: a save's R4 queued behind a background save), `S_cancel` (hangs, a stuck member, closed tabs), `S_recover_lock` (an earlier session's engine holding the engine lock at boot; short traces kept, so the boot's own queued job is cancelled behind the lock), `S_r4_fresh` (`S_r4_attempts` without crashes, every Cancel left out: saves finish and R4 runs in a fresh engine), `S_r4_attempts` (R4's crashes and the window), `S_queue_bound1` (`S_queue` with `StopBound` 1 and one window expiry, every Cancel left out: two queued jobs end without an engine at once, Try again). The constants come from the committed `.cfg` files, changed only where the table says. TLC walks 20 times the kept number of traces and keeps the first long enough ones (a world runs out of things to do early when the boot is cancelled). Size: 62 traces, 1,477 steps, 476 KB, replayed through both backends in about 0.6 s; enough that every mapped action but `OrphanEndStuck` occurs (its stuck orphan needs a quit or an app crash, both BL-0154): the rarest are `UserTryAgain` 3, `FreshEnd` and `SpawnStep` 6 each, `OrphanEnd` and `Hang` 8 each, `UserCloseTab` and `Stuck` 12 each, `DispatchUnavail` 14. Two worlds were added and one changed because mutants survived on narrower corpora (`S_queue_done` for X1, the short `S_recover_lock` traces for X2), and `LS_free_orphan` was dropped: its orphan needs a quit (left out), so it added nothing `S_cancel` lacks. Rejected: a larger corpus by default (the size buys coverage only where a mutant shows a hole).
- **Equivalences** (agent; the test's docstring): the model's `cancel` advice is the Cancel's own message, an effect in the code, and says nothing about the timer's advice; the shell may show the timer's advice before the model's `AdviceTimer` step while that step is enabled (the model's timer is a delay of any length; the shell's clock moves for every timer at once); a reset under way counts as unavailable. Not compared: the engine's number and the epoch (bookkeeping; what they decide is compared through the fresh-engine count and the advice), the engine lock's own state (the replay opens it when the model's orphan has ended), and the switch's window (the shell ends the boot's window when recover-all ends; the model at `Assess`, BL-0154; nothing can be queued in between, so dispatch, which is compared, cannot differ).
- **The fake engine** (agent): `FakeEngine.hold_ends` holds every end report until the next `release_ends()` and keeps holding (the model's `Drain` releases them), and `held` says how many are held; the existing `stuck` behaves as before.
- **Recover-all's work is played by the replay** (agent): `scheduler._recover_nothing` (the boot's recover-all until BL-0151) is replaced in the shell replay by a scripted work, so the model's recover-all steps (and a Cancel while it runs) have a job to act on.
- **The reader lives in the generator, the projection in the test** (agent): the corpus holds the model's raw values (only the variables read), so the projection and the equivalences sit beside the code they check.

### What the replay cannot see (by construction, each covered by hand-written tests)
- Branches the model never takes: a Cancel past the commit point ("too late": the model offers no Cancel there), an idle engine's death, a failed engine-lock wait and an incompatible engine (not in the model), a second background save or R4 (`AlreadyQueued`: the model never asks), `ENGINE_QUEUE_FULL` (no bound in the model), `WORKSPACE_NOT_OPEN` (the model never submits for a closed workspace), a job between engines while the engine is unavailable other than recover-all's (BL-0151).
- The crash's own drain: the model's engine death leaves no ending slot, so the replay drains a crashed engine at once.
