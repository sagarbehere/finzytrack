# S0170: Design foundations: the visual language as one theme, its spec and a gallery

Status: brief drafted by the coordinator on 2026-11-28 from the owner's design review; the implementer completes the brief (orient, acceptance tests, doc impact) and runs the slice.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-28, in session, verbatim)
The review ran on a Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Bne5cKpiuvWsuRbpCAcPCs (private to the owner; the chosen board is "v0.3 · 4 Synthesis", file `Synthesis.dc.html`).
- **A new look is allowed:** "We can take a new direction. We don't need to adhere to v0.3's look". This replaces ADR-0035's "Today's indigo/gray look is kept".
- **Rejected along the way:** option C, "I did not like C because to me it looks a bit like a toy (not serious) look"; v0.3 · 2, "I'm eliminating v0.3 2 because it looks .. just cluttered.."; a cream background with indigo, "I don't think that cream/beige colored background goes well with Indigo accents. I think the Indigo accents go well with the bluish/grayish/slate kind of background colors in v0.3 1 and v0.3 2"; a restyle that dropped columns, "No, that isn't good.. because I'll need all the information elements that are now missing in your update. Unroll your change".
- **Chosen:** "the v0.3 1 Refined's sidebar and top bar are to my liking. They also match the existing v0.3 that I like. I like the table of v0.3 3 over v0.3 1's table." The synthesis of those (the "v0.3 · 4 Synthesis" board) is the visual language.
- **Defaults:** "Yes, Balance links should be neutral, I think. Density comfortable. We want full support for Dark mode from Day 1". Balances are links to their transactions (owner: "the balances ARE links. Clicking on them takes one to corresponding transactions").
- **Visual language and information design are separate** (the coordinator's suggestion; owner: "I like your suggestions."): adopt the synthesis now as the look; each vertical's brief starts with a design step that shows the owner two versions on a canvas, "faithful" (v0.3's information in the new look) and "re-imagined" (in the spirit of the canvas's options A and B, the same capabilities, ADR-0043), and the owner picks per screen. Restyling changes presentation only; no information element v0.3 shows is dropped without the owner.
- **One theme file, no user theming:** the owner asked whether the visual language could be "the default "theme" rather than being baked in", adding "I don't think I or other users will spend time changing the theme.. it's a finance app after all, for serious users". The coordinator's recommendation, accepted ("Yes, take that into S0170's brief"): `theme.css` is the only place the look is defined; components use token names only; a lint rule refuses raw colours (hex, rgb/hsl/oklch literals, Tailwind palette classes such as `indigo-600`) outside it; the contrast tests run on the tokens in both modes; no theme switching beyond light/dark/system, no user theme files or editor.

## Brief (to complete)
- **Goal:** the chosen look as the app's default theme. (1) A visual-language spec in `design/specs/` (an Orientation block; the shell: sidebar, top bar, page header; the page templates the shell will use; tables: sentence-case headers, hairline separators, a card defined by a faint outline, right-aligned tabular amounts, one line per currency, neutral balance links with a dotted underline turning indigo on hover; filters: segmented period control, compact filter field, selects, toggles; badges; buttons; density comfortable; dark mode; voice for help, empty and error text, concise for non-native readers). (2) `frontend/src/app/theme.css` as the single source: colour, type scale (Inter), spacing, radius, shadow and density tokens, light and dark, the cool slate surfaces with the indigo accent as on the board. (3) The lint rule against raw colours outside `theme.css`, with must-pass and must-refuse tests. (4) Contrast tests over every token pair in both modes (extend `tests/unit/app/theme.test.ts`). (5) A dev-only gallery route showing the shell, a table, filters, badges, buttons, links and fields in both themes, excluded from the production build. (6) ADR-0035 amendment (the look; one theme file; no user theming) and the per-vertical design step written into PROCESS.md or roadmap §3 (both quote the owner as above).
