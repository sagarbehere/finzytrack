# Kernel: the Beancount domain vectors

## Orientation
- **What exists:** the vectors only (S0024): 62 cases in seven families (125 vectors: a transaction, an assertion or a booking per case, a name, code, query or currency per item), each an input plus what Beancount 3.2.0 says about it, generated by running Beancount and committed as data. No product code yet: the M1a engine wrappers (`ledger.beancount_port`, `ledger.grammar`) and the later frontend and main-sidecar ports must reproduce them (BL-0002).
- **Entry points:** `corpora/vectors/cases.toml` (the inputs; edit this); `corpora/vectors/vectors.json` (the vectors; written by `scripts/oracle/gen_domain_vectors.py --write`, never by hand); `scripts/oracle/vectors.py` (the checks).
- **Decisions it applies:** ADR-0024 (Beancount is the oracle; the frontend tolerance port reports "indeterminate" for postings that need booking; account grammar is the parser's, not `account.is_valid`; one PriceIndex with price-map semantics), ADR-0023 (display precision from `dcontext`; written scale kept), ADR-0025 (current values exclude future-dated entries), ADR-0031 (every booking method Beancount books), ADR-0004 (Beancount 3.2.0; an upgrade reruns the generator).
- **Invariants:** INV-02 (these are its vectors; it stays `pending` until the M1a code passes them on both sides), INV-03 (custom roots, every booking method), INV-23 (synthetic data only).
- **Traps:** the `*` tolerance default is not a floor: a currency that inferred a tolerance from its own digits ignores it (`tolerance/default-star-does-not-raise-inferred`). A priced or cost leg gives no tolerance to its price currency, so integer cash legs mean zero tolerance. An elided leg is rounded to the inferred precision (`6.790`, not `6.7897`). A balance assertion is checked at the start of its day. `get_price` with a date is inclusive and skips later prices; with no date it returns the latest price, future ones included, so "current" values must always pass today's date (ADR-0025). The transaction metadata `__tolerances__` is computed before booking; the balance check recomputes on the booked postings (what `tolerances` here holds). `beancount.core.account.is_valid` is not the grammar (`core_is_valid` shows where it is wrong). Beancount's error messages are informative: consumers compare verdicts and numbers, not message text.
- **Main tests:** `scripts/oracle/tests/test_domain_vectors.py` (fresh generation equals the committed file; every check passes; a must-not-pass test per check). Runs in the T0 pre-push hook and T1's `oracle-unit`.

## How a vector is made

Each case in `cases.toml` is hand-written; the generator renders it as a small ledger (options, an `open` on 2000-01-01 for every account used and not opened, the case's `setup`, then the item under test) and asks Beancount through its public functions: `loader.load_string`, `parser.parse_string`, `interpolate.infer_tolerances` and `compute_residual`, `balance.get_balance_tolerance`, `prices.build_price_map` and `get_price`, the display context. A case's `setup` must load with no errors, so a negative vector is negative because of the item under test. Each case or item declares a `polarity` (positive: Beancount accepts, balances, finds; negative: it refuses or finds nothing), and the checks refuse a polarity Beancount disagrees with. Each case cites the Beancount symbol that decides it (`beancount:<module>.<symbol>`, checked to exist) and the ADR or review section that asked for it.

Numbers are JSON strings in plain notation with Beancount's exponent kept (`10.50`, `0.0000000000000000000000001`); compare them by value unless the field is about written scale (`interpolated` units).

## The families

| Family | What it pins | Consumers |
|---|---|---|
| `tolerance` | One transaction: the inferred tolerance per currency and the default, the residual per currency (both on the booked postings, as `validate_check_transaction_balances` does), `balanced`, the leg Beancount fills when one is elided, whether Beancount dropped the transaction, and the frontend port's verdict | Engine: the tolerance wrapper behind `beancount_port` (from `input.ledger`). Frontend: `src/domain/tolerance.ts` (from `input.postings` and `input.tolerance_options`, as the Profile serves them) must reproduce `tolerances`, `residual` and `port` |
| `balance` | A balance assertion: its tolerance (explicit `~`, or 2 × multiplier at its written precision, 0 for an integer), the actual balance of the account and its children before that day, the difference, and Beancount's errors | Engine (the `balance_assertions` rows and reconciliation messages) |
| `account` | Whether a name is a valid account under the given root-name options, Beancount's errors, and `account.is_valid` for contrast | Engine: `ledger.grammar.parse_account(text, profile)` must equal `valid`. Frontend: no grammar port (ADR-0024 rejects it); a local precheck may accept a negative name but must never refuse a positive one |
| `currency` | Whether a code is a valid currency, probed as a `commodity` directive and as an amount's currency | Engine (Commodity construction). Frontend: same rule as accounts for any local precheck |
| `price` | Price-map lookups: as-of (inclusive), latest, inverse (1/price at 28 digits), same-day duplicates (the last in Beancount's entry order wins; when both directions exist the direction with fewer points is inverted into the other and, on a shared day, the inverted point wins), zero prices (no inverse), no chaining through a third currency, `implicit_prices` | Main sidecar: `domain.PriceIndex` and the `price_asof` UDF, fed from the mirror's `prices` rows (which include plugin prices), must reproduce every query |
| `precision` | Display precision per currency from `dcontext`: most common and maximum fractional digits; none for a currency Beancount never saw a number in | Engine: the Ledger Profile's per-commodity precision (ADR-0023); the frontend receives it and computes nothing |
| `booking` | Which lots a reduction books under each method (STRICT, STRICT_WITH_SIZE, NONE, AVERAGE, FIFO, LIFO, HIFO), the ledger option against the per-account method, a sale before a later lot, and Beancount's refusals | Engine: booking previews and the lot picker (ADR-0031, M8) read Beancount's booking; the vectors pin what they must show |

### The frontend port's verdict (ADR-0024)

The only rule here that is ours: a posting **needs booking** when any number or currency of its units, cost or price is left out (`{}` is a cost with every part left out; `{{1000 USD}}` parses as a per-unit 0 plus a total and is complete). The port's verdict is `indeterminate` when any posting needs booking, else `balanced` or `unbalanced` from Beancount's residual and tolerances. The checks also confirm that `balanced` agrees with Beancount's own "Transaction does not balance" error. A port computes weights as Beancount does: a `@@` total is divided by the absolute units to a per-unit price and multiplied back (28 significant digits, half-even), a `{{T}}` cost likewise; a cost leg weighs at cost and its price is ignored.

## Coverage

`vectors.COVERAGE` names, per family, each behaviour the review and the ADRs ask for (built from review:L3 §1.4 and §1.5.2, review:L3-verif §2.3–§2.5 and §3.2, ADR-0024, ADR-0023, ADR-0031 and the backlog's WS-KERNEL acceptance line): 13 for `tolerance`, 6 for `balance`, 9 for `account`, 6 for `currency`, 10 for `price`, 3 for `precision`, 9 for `booking`. The check fails when no vector shows one of them, and the test pins which vectors show each (`SHOWN_BY`), so a loosened behaviour check or a shrunk corpus is noticed.

## Left for later

- **Amount and date parsing** (review:L3 §1.4 `parse.json`; L3-11): not Beancount semantics but our parsers of bank formats (Dr suffixes, U+2212, `1.23E+05`); its shared corpus comes with WS-KERNEL's parsers and L4's readers.
- **Account names from a ledger whose `option "name_*"` line comes after the first use**: Beancount refreshes the roots as it reads options; the probe always puts options first. Add a vector when the Profile reads roots (M3).
- **Same-day prices across files** (the price sidecar, ADR-0030): Beancount orders entries by date, type and line number, not by file; the sidecar's merge rule comes with the PriceIndex (M7/M8).
- **Booking with labels, merges (`{*}`) and partial lot specs (`{2020-01-01}`)**: the lot-picker vectors with M8 (ADR-0031), on this generator.
- **Period balances against `bean-query`** (review:27 §M3): a separate golden over the corpus, with the D10 endpoint.
- **`infer_tolerance_from_cost` with a total cost or a price**: the generator handles them; no case pins them yet.
